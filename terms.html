Terms

Service limitations

Vehicle Report Desk provides research, evidence organization, and educational decision support. Reports are not mechanical inspections, appraisals, title guarantees, warranties, insurance advice, legal advice, or predictions of future reliability.

Independent verification

Vehicle histories and public sources may be incomplete. Buyers remain responsible for verifying identity, ownership, liens, title brands, odometer readings, recall status, insurance availability, and physical condition. A buyer-selected pre-purchase inspection remains essential.

No brokerage or transaction representation

We do not contact or negotiate with another party on a customer's behalf, locate or arrange a vehicle purchase, hold deposits, transfer titles, accept compensation tied to a vehicle sale, or represent either party in the transaction.

Seller-sponsored packets

Seller transparency packets are sponsored by the seller and organize supplied evidence. They are not independent certifications. Seller-reported claims remain labeled as such unless supported by identified documentation.

Third-party reports

We do not reproduce or resell consumer CARFAX or AutoCheck reports. Customers should obtain original history products directly from authorized providers and observe their applicable terms.

Payment and fulfillment

Payment is processed securely by Stripe. Work begins after payment and after we receive enough vehicle information to perform the requested research. Normal delivery is within two to three business days after the required information is complete; unusual records, missing evidence, or additional customer questions may extend that timeframe.

Electronic delivery

Completed reports are delivered electronically to the email address supplied with the order. Customers are responsible for providing and monitoring an accurate address, including spam or junk folders. Contact us promptly if a delivery message or attachment is unavailable.

Cancellations and refunds

You may request a full refund before research begins. After research begins, refunds are evaluated based on work already completed. If we cannot deliver the purchased report, we will issue a full refund. A completed custom report is not refundable merely because its findings are unfavorable or because the customer decides not to buy or sell the vehicle.

Customer responsibility

Customers are responsible for supplying accurate information and for obtaining permission to share any documents they provide. Vehicle Report Desk may pause or decline work involving suspected fraud, altered records, unlawful requests, or insufficient evidence.
