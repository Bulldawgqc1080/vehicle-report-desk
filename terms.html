Terms

Service limitations

Vehicle Report Desk provides research, evidence organization, and educational decision support. Reports are not mechanical inspections, appraisals, title guarantees, warranties, insurance advice, legal advice, or predictions of future reliability.

Independent verification

Vehicle histories and public sources may be incomplete. Buyers remain responsible for verifying identity, ownership, liens, title brands, odometer readings, recall status, insurance availability, and physical condition. A buyer-selected pre-purchase inspection remains essential.

No brokerage or transaction representation

We do not contact or negotiate with another party on a customer's behalf, locate or arrange a vehicle purchase, hold deposits, transfer titles, accept compensation tied to a vehicle sale, or represent either party in the transaction.

Seller-sponsored packets

Seller transparency packets are sponsored by the seller and organize supplied evidence. They are not independent certifications. Seller-reported claims remain labeled as such unless supported by identified documentation.

Third-party reports

We do not reproduce or resell consumer CARFAX or AutoCheck reports. Customers should obtain original history products directly from authorized providers and observe their applicable terms.
