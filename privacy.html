Privacy

Privacy notice

The website intake form does not transmit to or store information on our server. Submitting it opens your own email application with the information filled in.

Information you choose to send

If you email us, we receive the contact information, listing URL, VIN, documents, and questions you include. We use that information to respond to your request and prepare the requested report.

Sensitive information

Do not send unredacted driver licenses, payment-card data, Social Security numbers, financial-account information, or title documents showing personal addresses. Redact personal information before sending vehicle records.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe-hosted checkout pages. Vehicle Report Desk does not receive or store your full payment-card number. Stripe receives payment and contact information under its own privacy policy and provides us with transaction details needed to confirm and fulfill the order.

Third-party links

Sample PDFs and research sources may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are their own.
