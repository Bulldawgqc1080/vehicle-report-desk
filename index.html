Common questions

What this service can—and cannot—do.

Is this a mechanical inspection?

No. Reports turn available evidence into specific inspection targets, but a qualified buyer-selected shop must inspect the vehicle physically.

Does the report include CARFAX or AutoCheck?

No. We do not reproduce or resell consumer history reports. You may supply a report for review, and buyers should obtain original history information directly from an authorized provider.

How long does it take?

Normal delivery is within two business days after payment and complete vehicle information are received. The delivery clock starts when the required intake is complete; we will contact you if missing or unusual records delay research.

What if the listing disappears?

Send any saved screenshots, VIN, price, mileage, seller claims, and records you have. We will confirm whether there is enough evidence to proceed; if not, you may cancel for a full refund before research begins.

Can I ask a follow-up question?

Yes. Reply within seven calendar days of delivery. Your purchase includes one follow-up response answering questions about the original report and same vehicle. Materially new evidence, a replacement vehicle, or additional research may require a new order.

Can you guarantee the title is clean or the vehicle is reliable?

No. History sources can be incomplete, and no research can predict every future failure. Material unknowns remain labeled unknown.

Will you negotiate or contact the other party?

No. We provide research and decision support only. We do not arrange purchases, contact sellers on a buyer’s behalf, negotiate, take deposits, or earn commission from a vehicle sale.

What is the seller’s Vehicle Highlights Sheet?

It is a concise, seller-sponsored handout built from the information supplied for the kit. It is not a certification. Buyers should still verify title, history, and physical condition independently.
