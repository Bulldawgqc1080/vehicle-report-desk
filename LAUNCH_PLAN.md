# Customer Acquisition Plan

## Strongest first channel: inspection and automotive-service partners

Start with independent mobile mechanics, pre-purchase inspection shops, detailers, listing photographers, and specialty repair shops in the East Valley. They already meet buyers and sellers at the exact moment this service is useful.

Offer partners:

- A co-branded sample packet
- A QR card they can hand customers
- Reciprocal referrals without compensation tied to a vehicle sale
- A report that turns their inspection findings into a buyer-readable document

Avoid dealer-placement agreements, vehicle-finding, negotiation, transaction arranging, and payment tied to whether a vehicle sells.

## First 30-day validation

1. Complete 10 reports at founding-customer pricing:
   - Buyer report: $49
   - Seller packet: $39
2. Require permission before using any anonymized excerpt or testimonial.
3. Track: inquiry source, paid conversion, turnaround time, refund/request rate, and which report sections customers mention.
4. Raise to published pricing ($89 / $69) after five paid reports and two usable testimonials.

## Direct channels, in priority order

### 1. Local automotive partners

Contact 20 shops personally with both sample PDFs. Ask for feedback before asking for referrals. The pitch is not “sell this for me”; it is “this makes your inspection easier for customers to understand.”

### 2. Helpful local content

Publish short, anonymized listing teardowns:

- “Three things missing from this $15,000 Marketplace listing”
- “Why no open recall does not prove a repair was completed”
- “The invoice you need after ‘internal transmission repaired’”
- “Seller packet: what a serious buyer actually wants to see”

End with one call to action: submit the listing for a report.

### 3. Local Facebook and enthusiast groups

Ask administrators before posting. Lead with useful education and disclosed sample reports—not cold DMs or repetitive promotion. Do not scrape members or mass-message Marketplace sellers.

### 4. Search-intent pages

Create focused pages after the first sales prove the offer:

- Used car buyer report
- Facebook Marketplace car research
- Private-party seller transparency packet
- Used car inspection checklist Arizona

### 5. Google Search Ads

Test only after the landing page converts warm referrals. Begin with exact/high-intent queries and a strict daily cap. Do not create a Google Business Profile unless the service makes genuine in-person customer contact; online-only businesses are ineligible.

## Lead offer

Do not give away the full research. Offer a free automated/brief **Evidence Checklist** that tells prospects which five items to gather. The paid report performs the actual research, reconciliation, price screen, and decision support.

## Weekly scorecard

- Partner conversations
- Qualified inquiries
- Paid reports
- Conversion rate
- Average turnaround time
- Buyer vs. seller mix
- Customer objections
- Testimonials/case studies authorized

The goal is not traffic. The goal is five paid strangers who say the report changed how they handled a real vehicle.
